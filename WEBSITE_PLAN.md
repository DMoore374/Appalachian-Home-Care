# website Strategy: Appalachian Home Care

## 1. Website Strategy Overview
**Primary Purpose:** To serve as a digital "front porch" that converts anxious family members and seniors into scheduled care consultations.

*   **Primary Conversion Goal:** Scheduled In-Home Care Consultation.
*   **Secondary Conversion Goals:** Phone calls (Click-to-Call), Contact form submissions, Caregiver applications.
*   **Ideal Visitor Types:**
    *   **The "Anxious Daughter":** Living 3 hours away, worried about Mom falling.
    *   **The "Overwhelmed Spouse":** Needs a break (Respite) but feels guilty.
    *   **The "Direct Senior":** Wants to stay home but needs help with laundry/bathing.
    *   **The "Professional Referrer":** Discharge planners needing a reliable partner.
*   **Emotional vs. Practical Problems:**
    *   **Emotional:** Fear of the unknown, guilt of not doing enough, anxiety about safety.
    *   **Practical:** How to pay for care, how to vetting caregivers, scheduling consistency.
*   **Speed of Response:** In home care, the first company to answer the phone usually wins the client because the need is often urgent (post-hospitalization).
*   **Local Positioning:** Position as "Neighbors helping Neighbors." Not a franchise, but a part of the Appalachian community.

## 3. Homepage Wireframe

1.  **Hero Section:** High-quality image of a caregiver and senior laughing in a local-style home. Headline focused on "Staying Home Safely."
2.  **Trust Bar:** Logos of local chambers, Better Business Bureau, and "Licensed by State Health Dept."
3.  **Problem/Pain Section:** "The weight of caregiving shouldn't stay on your shoulders alone."
4.  **How We Help (Services):** 4 Cards: Personal Care, Companion, Respite, Specialized.
5.  **How It Works (3 Steps):** 1. Connect (Call/Form) -> 2. Consult (In-Home) -> 3. Care (Personalized Plan).
6.  **"Appalachian Roots" Section:** Our story of local commitment.
7.  **Testimonials:** Scrolled "Stories from local families."
8.  **Final CTA:** "Because Family Deserves the Best. Schedule your free consultation."

## 5. Service Page Copy Framework (Repeatable)
*   **Headline:** [Service Name] Designed for [Specific Need]
*   **Emotional Opening:** Focus on the relief the family feels knowing this is handled.
*   **Who it’s for:** Bulleted list of scenarios.
*   **What’s Included:** Specific tasks (meal prep, grooming, light housekeeping).
*   **Signs you need this:** "They are wearing the same clothes for days," "The fridge is empty."
*   **CTA:** "Start your customized care plan today."

---

## 6. Conversion Strategy
*   **Sticky Mobile Header:** Always shows a "Call Now" and "Schedule" button.
*   **Lead Magnet:** "The Appalachian Guide to Caring for Senior Parents" (PDF in exchange for email).
*   **Trust Signals:** Caregiver background check badge, "100% Local" badge.
*   **Booking:** Integration with Calendly for direct consultation booking.

---

## 7. AI Receptionist Strategy
*   **Greeting:** "Hi there! I'm the Appalachian Home Care assistant. Are you looking for care for yourself, a loved one, or looking for a career with us?"
*   **Urgency Check:** "Is this an urgent need for care following a hospital discharge?"
*   **Escalation:** "If this is a medical emergency, please dial 911 immediately."

## 8. SEO Plan
*   **Primary Keywords:** Home care [City], Personal care [City], Senior care services.
*   **Local SEO:** Specific pages for every county/major town served.
*   **Schema:** LocalBusiness and FAQ schema for Google Rich Snippets.

## 9. Visual Design Direction (Appalachian Modern)
*   **Palette:** Forest Green (#2D5A27), Warm Earth (#A67B5B), Antique White (#F9F7F2).
*   **Typography:** Playfair Display (Headers), Inter (Body).
*   **Imagery:** Authentic, non-stock-looking photos of senior-caregiver interactions.
